## 提示词

Create a production macOS dark app icon raster asset, square 1024x1024. Image 1 is the existing app icon and identity/geometry target. Image 2 is a style reference only. Preserve Image 1's exact open door silhouette, circular door knob, rounded rectangular door frame and right-pointing exit arrow, composition and proportions. Change the white door, frame, knob and arrow to saturated luminous purple-violet with tasteful smooth gradient and subtle bevels like image 2. Replace purple background with nearly black charcoal gradient, gentle lighting at upper left, very restrained purple ambient shadow. IMPORTANT full-bleed square dark background reaching all four corners, NO rounded outer tile, NO white border, NO margin or white corners; macOS handles masking. Clean premium subtle 3D material, crisp legible silhouette at small sizes, no text, no watermark. Keep the small door knob from image 1. Output a single finished icon, no mockup.
